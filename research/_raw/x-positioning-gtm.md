  tied to a specific disclosed case).

  environment. It's just so different." (attributed to @jasonlk, 2026-07-31; 15 words.)

  vs. budget-capture); go-to-market-and-launch (hiring/motion fit).

  backed by disclosed data. Low engagement (17 likes) — not treated as a credibility signal per the
