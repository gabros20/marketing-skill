## 1. Verdict and wedge

**The niche is NOT open — first family build with a structural peer at scale.**
`coreyhaines31/marketingskills`: 42,544★ (2026-08-01, API-verified), MIT, 49 skills / 150 refs /

Around it ~700+ marketing-labeled skills across 20+ repos. Every candidate job is already covered
somewhere, several redundantly.

**The wedge survived, re-argued. Four planks, each evidence-backed:**

- **P1 — Adjudication.** Across 11 cloned packs the Ehrenberg-Bass canon exists in exactly ONE file
  — a roleplay persona dossier (Byron Sharp racked beside Russell Brunson), never connected to the
  attribution mechanics; ESOV and 95-5 appear nowhere. Nothing in the genre distinguishes a
  replicated law from a personality's heuristic. Our pack makes evidence-grading an operating
  default and *connects* brand science to measurement.
- **P2 — Honest limits.** The field's own most-quoted numbers are folklore (95-5 is a disclaimed
  arithmetic heuristic; the ESOV coefficient is unresolvable; B2B 46/54 has no locatable primary),
  and the rigorous laws' domain of validity stops short of our default reader (no SaaS panel
  dataset exists; zero-audience n=1 is outside every dataset in both directions). Nobody in the
  ecosystem says "you cannot measure this at your scale." We teach what remains.
- **P3 — Checkable primary strips, done right.** Deliverability (RFC 8058 / spam-rate / sender
  rules: 0 hits in the incumbent), the four consent regimes, store/marketplace listing rules,
  Google's own docs killing four SEO myths, the llms.txt falsification (4-way convergence). Plus
  live staleness specimens in high-adoption packs (Play title 50-vs-30; OKF v0.1-vs-v0.2; FAQ
  schema removal) — the anti-staleness invariant with citable examples.
- **P4 — Seam discipline.** All 11 surveyed packs blend marketing with sales/growth/success/
  frontend (one self-reports 28% marketing; one ships literal UI components). We are the only pack
  in the genre inside a family with declared seams. Must be argued to the reader, not assumed
  (see R5, AARRR).

**Storefront rule (automation lesson):** the wedge is for SOURCES.md and this gate. The front door
says what the pack IS — positioning, launch, SEO, email, ads, content, ASO, measurement for a
digital product — subject → scope → distinctive, written for someone who never read the lifecycle doc.

## 4. Honesty rulings (the pack's epistemics)

- **R10 — Specimen vs evidence is a taught pattern**, with the randfish case (best zero-click data
  + author sells the thesis) as the worked example. The ethics table (R6) and this rule are the
  pack's character.
- **R11 — Anti-staleness invariant** (ai-skill precedent): every volatile fact ships with as-of
  date + the check that re-verifies it; teach the check, not the list. Citable specimens: FAQ
  rich-results removal (2026-05-07), OKF v0.1→v0.2 in the incumbent, Play-title 50-vs-30 in a
  23.5k★ pack.
- **R12 — Positioning is craft.** Dunford's five components taught as widely-adopted practitioner
  craft with no empirical validation — her own framing. Never dress it as science.
- **R13 — Teach the laws WITH their domain boundary.** EB laws: strongest empirical base in
  marketing AND untested on SaaS/subscription; n=1 outside all datasets. Present the Thomaz
  dispute; don't settle it. 95-5 taught as the *idea* (most of the category isn't buying now) with
  its derivation and disclaimer visible.
- **R14 — The load-bearing claim:** a solo/small-spend builder can use neither platform
  attribution (over-claims by construction), nor a valid lift test (Lewis & Rao power math), nor
  MMM (vendor-documented 2–3yr weekly-data floors). What remains: UTM discipline, self-reported
  attribution, geo-split lift where geography allows, and pre-committed decision rules — the same
  independence move as automation's pre-committed-rule resolution (cite by name, never bare number).
- **R16 — The incumbent is treated with respect**: named in SOURCES.md with license; its
  attribution file acknowledged as the benchmark; wedge stated as discipline + integration, never
  disparagement. Its unsourced llms.txt claim is cited as a *specimen of the genre problem*, tied
  to the primary-source refutation.
